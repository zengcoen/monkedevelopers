Fruit Box Solver

Stuck on a packed Fruit Box board? Fruit Box Solver finds every valid box of fruits that adds up to the target so you can clear the grid and keep your streak going.

  • Photo Capture & Upload: Snap a picture of your Fruit Box board or upload an existing screenshot for analysis.
  • Instant Box Finder: Advanced algorithms scan the grid and highlight every grouping that sums to the target.
  • Clear-the-grid Guidance: See the best boxes first so you can wipe the board without missing a combination.

Whether you’re chasing a high score or just stuck on a crowded board, Fruit Box Solver is your go-to app for finishing every puzzle.
