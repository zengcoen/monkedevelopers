Privacy Policy

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-11.

Introduction

Net Worth Calculator is a free planning app made by Monke Developers. This policy explains what information the app uses. In short, the app does not collect personal information, and it does not send any data off your device.

Information We Collect

Net Worth Calculator does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or enter personal details. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The financial figures you enter, such as income, expenses, savings, goals, and age, are used only to make projections inside the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves your scenarios and settings on your device, so they are still there the next time you open the app. This includes the figures you enter and whether you have seen the introduction screens. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it by deleting the app.

Exporting Your Plan

When you choose Export CSV or Export Text, the app creates a file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file is sent only where you choose to send it. The app does not upload exported files to our servers.

Network Use

The app works fully offline. It does not connect to our servers or to any third-party service to calculate your projections.

Analytics, Advertising, and Purchases

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or in-app purchases. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect personal information. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because the app does not collect or send personal information, there is no personal data on our side to protect. Your saved data stays on your device under the control of your operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, so please review it periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email monkedevelopers@gmail.com.
