Strands Solver

Stuck on today’s NYT Strands grid? Strands Solver reads the letters, finds theme words, and highlights the spanning pangram.

  • Photo Capture & Upload: Snap the letter grid or upload a screenshot.
  • Theme Words & Spangram: See suggested paths for the theme words and the word that spans the puzzle.
  • Manual Tweaks: Correct any misread letters, then step through the solution on the board.

Whether you’re hunting the last theme word or the spangram, Strands Solver is your go-to app for clearing the grid.
