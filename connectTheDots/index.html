Connect the Dots

Have a photo you want to draw? Connect the Dots traces the main subject and turns it into a numbered outline you can print or save.

  • Photo Capture & Upload: Snap a person, pet, or object, or pick a picture from your library.
  • Numbered Outline: Get a clean connect-the-dots page with the title “Connect the Dots.”
  • Print-Ready Pages: Save or print in Letter, A4, or Legal, portrait or landscape.

Whether you want a rainy-day worksheet or a keepsake from a favorite photo, Connect the Dots is your go-to app for turning pictures into drawings.
